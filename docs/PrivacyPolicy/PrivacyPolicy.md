# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 24, 2025_

Hidden Sri Lanka ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Name, email address, and other information you provide during registration or authentication.

### Location Data
- We collect your device's location to provide location-based services, such as showing nearby attractions and navigation features.

### Usage Data
- Information about how you use the app, including features accessed, time spent, and error logs.

### Images and Media
- Photos you upload or share within the app, including those from Google Photos integration.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and improve app functionality
- To personalize your experience

- To display relevant attractions and navigation
- To communicate important updates or information
- For analytics and troubleshooting

## 3. Information Sharing and Disclosure
- We do not sell or rent your personal information.
- We may share information with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., Firebase, Google APIs) only as necessary to operate the app.
- We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

## 4. Data Security
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
- Access to your information is restricted to authorized personnel only.

## 5. Your Choices
- You can update or delete your account information at any time Please Contact Us .

## 6. Children's Privacy
- The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
